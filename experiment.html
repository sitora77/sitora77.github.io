What the results do—and do not—show

Baseline policies can coincide. If cost-only and joint-risk allocations are the same, the measured improvement is zero. This model does not guarantee that a more complex method will outperform a simpler policy.

The full report includes paired bootstrap intervals, training-seed stability, sample-size diagnostics, equations, input hashes and exact unrounded values. The MPA arrivals series is displayed separately as public-data context.
